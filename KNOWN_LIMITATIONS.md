# KNOWN LIMITATIONS & RISK DISCLOSURES

1. **Simulated Execution Only**:
   - The platform strictly executes simulated paper trades within its internal double-entry ledger. It intentionally does NOT connect to live brokerage execution endpoints or place real-money orders.
2. **Data Provider Latency**:
   - Unauthenticated / free tier Yahoo Finance feeds are subject to approximately 15 minutes of delay during active market hours. All such quotes are explicitly badged as `DELAYED`.
3. **Options Chain Liquidity**:
   - Historical minute-level options tick data is computationally intensive and subject to provider limitations. The platform calculates Black-Scholes Greeks dynamically based on available snapshots and does not claim to backtest minute-level option fills on historical expirations unless historical option chain archives are provided.
4. **Regulatory Classification**:
   - The signals, scores, and probabilities generated by this platform represent algorithmic research tools and do not constitute SEBI-registered investment advice or research analyst recommendations.
